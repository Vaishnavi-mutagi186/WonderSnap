Gesture Control Guide

🖐️ Open Palm
Expands Particles: Spread all 5 fingers to trigger radial cosmic expansion and accelerate particles outward.
✊ Closed Fist
Gravity Collapse: Curl fingers tightly into a fist to generate a center black-hole attraction force.
👌 Pinch
Particle Scale & Zoom: Bring thumb tip and index fingertip together. The distance dynamically modulates particle size and camera zoom.
✌️ Two Fingers
Switch 3D Formations: Extend index and middle fingers (peace sign) to cycle seamlessly between Galaxy, Fireworks, Wave, Heart, and Spiral.
🔄 Hand Movement
3D Orbit & Tilt: Glide your hand left, right, up, or down across the camera frame to rotate the particle universe.
👐 Dual Hands
Spatial Warp: Place both hands in camera view. Moving them apart or closer scales and stretches the particle coordinates.
